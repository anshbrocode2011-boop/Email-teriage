Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance Statement

Email teriage's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Email teriage ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our"), hosted at https://email-teriage-3.onrender.com. Email teriage is an AI-powered email productivity assistant designed to help professionals synthesize, prioritize, and manage unread email in their Gmail inboxes.

We understand that email contains highly private and sensitive personal and professional communications. We have designed our software from the ground up to follow a strict principle of data minimization, transient in-memory processing, and absolute zero permanent retention of email content.

2. Google Account Data We Access (Requested Scopes)

When you authenticate using Google Sign-In and authorize the application, Email teriage requests access strictly to the following Google OAuth 2.0 scope:

Scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly

What We Collect & Access:

  • Email Message Headers: Sender Name, Sender Email Address, Date/Timestamp, and Subject Line.
  • Email Message Identifiers: Gmail internal message ID and thread ID (required to query the message via the official Gmail API).
  • Email Snippets & Body Text: Plain text snippet or body text of unread messages in your inbox.
  • Basic Account Identity: Your Google account email address and display name (used solely to show which account is currently connected in the UI).
What We NEVER Access or Request:
  • We do NOT request or have access to your account password or Google account security credentials.
  • We do NOT request write, compose, edit, or send permissions (gmail.compose, gmail.send, or gmail.modify).
  • We do NOT delete emails, move emails to trash, or alter your labels.
  • We do NOT access your Google Contacts, address books, Google Drive files, Docs, Sheets, or Google Calendar.
  • We do NOT scan sent emails, draft messages, or trash folders.

3. Purpose and How We Use Your Google User Data

We use data obtained through the Gmail API strictly and exclusively to provide explicit, user-facing productivity features inside the Email teriage interface:

Priority Classification

Categorizing unread emails into actionable priority tiers: Urgent, Worth a Look, Can Wait, and Ignore/Newsletter to help you focus on what matters.

One-Sentence TL;DR Summaries

Synthesizing lengthy email threads and newsletters into single, rapid 15-word summaries so you can triage dozens of messages in seconds.

Action Item Extraction

Identifying concrete deadlines, pending invoices, requested meetings, and specific tasks requested by senders.

Daily Executive Briefing

Compiling an aggregated executive overview of your unread messages upon explicit user request.

4. Data Storage, Retention, and Ephemeral Architecture

Strict Zero-Permanent-Storage Policy for Email Content:

Email teriage processes all email content ephemerally in server runtime memory (RAM). We do NOT save, log, persist, or store your raw email contents, message bodies, subject lines, or snippets on any hard disk, permanent database (such as SQL or NoSQL), or long-term file storage.

As soon as an email analysis request completes and the summary is sent to your active web browser, the server-side memory buffer is deallocated.

Authentication Token Handling: Temporary OAuth 2.0 access tokens granted by Google are held purely in your browser's sessionStorage during your active session. They are immediately cleared when you click "Disconnect & Sign Out" or when you close the browser tab.

5. Prohibition on Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) Model Training

Explicit AI / ML Model Training Policy:

Email teriage does NOT use Google user data (including email contents, subjects, sender information, or metadata) to develop, improve, fine-tune, or train generalized artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models.

Email analysis is processed strictly via private, enterprise-grade Google Gemini API calls configured with zero-retention parameters, ensuring your personal text is never incorporated into public AI training datasets.

6. Data Sharing, Disclosures & Third-Party Transfers

We uphold the highest standard of data confidentiality:

  • Zero Sale or Commercial Monetization: We do NOT sell, rent, lease, or trade personal data or Google user data to anyone, under any circumstances.
  • No Transfer to Data Brokers or Advertisers: Google user data is NEVER transferred to advertising networks, consumer profiling companies, data brokers, or marketing intermediaries.
  • No Advertising: We do NOT use Google user data to serve targeted advertisements, personalized promotions, or behavioral retargeting.
  • No Human Inspection: No human employees, engineers, or third-party contractors are permitted to read or inspect your email contents, unless required by legal subpoena or explicitly requested by you in writing for technical debugging.
  • Essential Service Providers: Data is only transmitted across encrypted channels to our hosting infrastructure (Render / Google Cloud) strictly to run the web server code requested by you.

7. User Control: Revoking Access and Data Deletion

You have full and perpetual control over Email teriage's access to your Google Account:

Method 1: In-App Disconnect

Click the "Disconnect & Sign Out" button located in the top-right corner of the application dashboard at any time. This immediately purges all stored tokens from your browser session.

Method 2: Google Account Security Dashboard

You can instantly revoke authorization directly at Google's official permissions manager: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions → Find Email teriage in the list of third-party apps and click "Remove Access".

Method 3: Direct Data Deletion Inquiries

Because we do not maintain a permanent database of your email content, there is no persistent email data to delete upon departure. However, you may email us at anshbrocode2011@gmail.com or support@inboxtriage.app for immediate audit verification. All requests are processed within 24 to 48 hours.

8. Security Standards and Safeguards

Email teriage employs industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect your information:

  • Encryption in Transit: All web traffic and API calls between your browser, our servers, and Google APIs use Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption).
  • Strict Least Privilege: The application only asks for the bare minimum read-only scope (gmail.readonly) required to parse unread messages.
  • Modern Isolated Cloud Environments: Our infrastructure runs within secure containerized instances on certified cloud platforms.
